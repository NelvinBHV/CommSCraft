# Evidence for the open points (O) and remaining gaps (L) – status 2026-10-26

For every open point of the conflict/gap list the search went in this order:
1. **normative**: Microsoft Open Specifications and IETF/ITU documents (file 38),
2. **official**: Microsoft documentation outside the Open Specifications (Microsoft Learn, Lync 2013 SDK reference, Download Center), ITU-T,
3. **secondary**: open-source implementations or observation notes (reverse engineering). These are **not specifications**. They confirm or refute an inference and stay "secondary evidence" until the owner's interop test. **No code was taken** from them, only behaviour described.

Downloaded documents and source archives are kept outside the repository; their SHA-256 values are in [`../sources-secondary.tsv`](../sources-secondary.tsv). Patent texts were read for understanding only and are not published (decision D-04).

## Not resolvable without interop or further paid standards

- **O-CP-1** (byte order), **O-QE-1** (SfB MOS method), **O-EX-3** (field contents of conversation history items), **O-EX-4/L-15** (voicemail list), **L-07** (SMPTE 421M, paid), **L-13** (SfB-specific transfer headers), **O-TU-1** (attribute 0x8006), **L-10/L-12/L-17** (references to available specifications or a renderer – not an evidence problem).
- RTAudio (x-msrta): no public bitstream description; Microsoft requires a licence. **N stays.**
