> **Last reviewed:** 2026-11-07 · **Last updated:** 2026-11-07 · **Change:** new

separate from the source-code licence: `MIT OR Apache-2.0` does not cover them (Apache-2.0 §6 also
grants no rights in names and marks). This is not legal advice.

## Provenance

The original artwork was generated with ChatGPT for the project owner; the vector files derive from
its concept, the small icon is a simplification of them. It is not presented as human-made, is not
derived from Microsoft or Skype artwork, and CommSCraft is not affiliated with or endorsed by
Microsoft.

## What you may do

To the extent anyone holds rights in the logo, you may, free of charge and worldwide:

- copy and show the unmodified logo to refer to CommSCraft (articles, documentation, talks, links);
- use it for unmodified builds and for distribution packages of CommSCraft, including resizing and
  format conversion.

## What you may not do

- Suggest that a modified version or another product is CommSCraft or is endorsed by the project.
  A modified fork chooses its own name and logo; "based on CommSCraft" is fine.
- Suggest an affiliation with Microsoft.

- **No exclusivity, no guarantee.** The artwork is AI-generated. OpenAI's terms assign its rights in
  output "if any" and state that other users may receive similar output. A German court denied

  München, judgment of 13 February 2026). The logo may therefore not be protected by copyright, and
  similar images may exist. The files are provided as they are, without any warranty of
  non-infringement.
